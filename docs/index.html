FATHOM

Helpful. Not omniscient. · Hear it

Every file is the real thing: the original Subnautica PDA voice, IVONA Amy through the original filter chain, in the register of the Alterra PDA: readings, verdicts and paperwork in one flat voice, whatever the reading is. Model lines were written live by a local model held to that register. Template lines play instantly from the cache. Pooled lines are for the quiet minutes. Headphones. Lights off.
